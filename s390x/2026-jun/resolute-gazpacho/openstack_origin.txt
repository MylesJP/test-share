# Sunbeam/OpenStack provenance

This file replaces the legacy charm 'openstack-origin' dump. Sunbeam
charms generally do not expose an 'openstack-origin' config option, so
the useful provenance is the selected Ubuntu/OpenStack series, Juju charm
channels/revisions/bases, and machine-side snap/package evidence.
Operational status is intentionally left to juju_status.txt.
